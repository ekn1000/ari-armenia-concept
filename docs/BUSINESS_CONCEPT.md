# ARMMATE business concept

## Positioning

**Your friend in Armenia.**

ARMMATE is a personal travel assistance service for tourists visiting Armenia. A visitor can explore independently while having a reliable local assistant to turn to throughout their agreed service period.

ARMMATE is not a traditional tour agency and is not simply an excursion booking platform. Recommendations and itineraries are part of assistance, not the whole product. A visitor does not have to hire a guide or book a packaged tour to use ARMMATE.

Suggested supporting line: “Your personal assistant in Armenia. Local support, useful recommendations and a helping hand whenever you need it.” Contact hours, response expectations and scope must be agreed individually; this sentence is not a promise of round-the-clock availability.

## The customer's problem

A visitor may not know the local language, customs, services, transport options or whom to call when something unexpected happens. Searching for separate services and contacts takes time and can make an independent trip feel difficult.

ARMMATE offers one trusted local contact who understands the visitor's needs and helps find practical solutions. The visitor keeps control of their trip and chooses when support or companionship is useful.

## Main service: online assistance

Each customer receives a personal assistant's contact on WhatsApp or Telegram after the offer is agreed, confirmed and paid according to the agreed terms.

The assistant can help with:

| Area                          | Examples                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Travel and recommendations    | Places to visit, restaurants, cafés, cultural experiences and itineraries suited to the visitor's interests and budget   |
| Transportation                | Finding taxis, private drivers, airport transfers and transport between cities                                           |
| Reservations and organization | Restaurant reservations, hotel recommendations, event tickets, entertainment and local services                          |
| Everyday needs                | Translation, communication, local customs, shopping, finding particular products or services and organizational problems |
| Unexpected situations         | Lost belongings, hotel or transport difficulties, communication problems and finding an appropriate local service        |

The responsibility is to help the customer find a practical way forward, not merely answer a question. ARMMATE does not guarantee an outcome controlled by an outside provider.

## Optional service: Personal Companion

A customer can separately arrange physical accompaniment for selected hours or days. This can include walks around Yerevan, interesting places, restaurants and cafés, shopping, communication with local people, local culture or simply company in an unfamiliar place.

Personal Companion is not necessarily a traditional guided tour. The customer decides whether they need it, on which days, for how many hours and what they want to do together. The scope, availability and price are agreed separately.

Where possible, the same personal assistant should accompany the customer throughout the stay to preserve trust and continuity. This is subject to availability, not a guarantee of the same person in every circumstance.

## Services through local partners

ARMMATE can help arrange airport transfers, private drivers, excursions, restaurants, hotels and other accommodation, tickets, entertainment and other local services.

These may have separate costs. An offer must clearly distinguish:

- ARMMATE's assistance fee.
- Any agreed personal companion cost.
- Third-party charges, such as drivers, hotels, tickets or meals.

No wording should imply that transport, accommodation or third-party services are free or automatically included.

## Individual pricing

There are no fixed packages, mandatory bundles or predefined public prices. Do not add pricing cards, “from” prices, hourly price promises or automatic price calculation at this stage.

Every customer receives a personalized offer after discussing:

- Length of stay in Armenia.
- Type and expected amount of assistance.
- Online support requirements and agreed contact hours.
- Whether personal companionship is needed.
- Number of companion hours or days.
- Additional services and individual requirements.

Pricing is handled manually. The customer pays according to the agreed requirements. ARMMATE's fee and third-party charges must be identifiable before service confirmation.

Website message: “Every journey is different. So is our assistance.”

Primary calls to action: “Get your personal offer”, “Request individual pricing” and “Contact ARMMATE”.

## Customer journey

1. Discover ARMMATE on the website.
2. Understand online assistance, optional companionship and individual pricing.
3. Contact ARMMATE with travel dates and requirements.
4. Discuss the customer's needs personally.
5. Receive an individual offer with the agreed scope and costs.
6. Confirm and pay according to the agreed terms.
7. Receive the personal assistant's contact information.
8. Contact the assistant as needed during the agreed service hours and period.
9. Arrange optional Personal Companion services separately if desired.
10. Continue assistance through the end of the agreed service period.

The website presents this in three friendly steps: share your trip; agree your personal offer; explore with support.

## Service expectations

ARMMATE provides personal communication, tailored recommendations, organizational help, local service search, reservation assistance, practical support with everyday unexpected situations and optional physical accompaniment.

Each customer's agreement defines service dates, contact channels, contact hours, response expectations, scope, companion availability and charges. There is no unconditional 24/7 promise.

ARMMATE does not promise:

- Guaranteed availability or performance of third-party services.
- Free transport or accommodation.
- Unlimited physical accompaniment.
- Emergency medical assistance, rescue or emergency response.
- Around-the-clock availability unless separately agreed.

## Website structure and language

The website remains in Armenian, Russian and English, with equivalent meaning and clear language controls. Its structure is:

1. Main proposition and personal-offer CTA.
2. What ARMMATE is and why a local contact helps.
3. Online assistance, optional companion and partner services.
4. How it works in three steps.
5. Individual pricing without fixed amounts.
6. Armenia inspiration framed as examples of support, not bookable packages.
7. Clear service expectations.
8. Personal enquiry preparation and official contact channels.
9. FAQ and photo credits.

The design keeps the established warm colours, Armenian landscapes, readable spacing, responsive layout and direct calls to action. Avoid invented testimonials, response-time promises, availability claims or fabricated contact details.

## Current implementation boundary

The website is a static, free-hosted service preview. It prepares a personalized enquiry locally and supports copying or downloading the message. Verified WhatsApp and Telegram contacts have not been supplied, so no live destination is configured and the preview states that clearly.

When real business contacts are configured, visitors can open a messenger with a prepared draft and confirm sending themselves. ARMMATE then handles the conversation, pricing and confirmation manually. The website does not accept payments or create bookings.

Operational terms, cancellation arrangements, actual hours, response expectations, partner arrangements and payment methods must be set by ARMMATE before taking paying customers. Do not invent these decisions in the website.
